📐 CVP Formula Sheet

Review these formulas before you start. You can reopen this sheet at any time during the simulation by clicking the 📐 Formulas button in the bottom-right corner.

Key Variables:  P = Selling Price per unit  |  VC = Variable Cost per unit  |  FC = Total Fixed Costs  |  Q = Units Sold  |  CM = Contribution Margin  |  NOI = Net Operating Income

📊 Contribution Margin

Contribution Margin formulas
CM per UnitCM = P − VC
CM RatioCM Ratio = CM per Unit ÷ P
Total CMTotal CM = Q × CM per Unit
VC RatioVC Ratio = 1 − CM Ratio

⚖ Break-Even Point

Break-Even Point formulas
Break-Even (units)BEP = FC ÷ CM per Unit
Break-Even (sales $)BEP = FC ÷ CM Ratio
NOI at any volumeNOI = (Q × CM per Unit) − FC
Using CM RatioNOI = (Sales $ × CM Ratio) − FC

🎯 Target Profit

Target Profit formulas
Required unitsQ = (FC + Target Profit) ÷ CM per Unit
Required sales $Sales $ = (FC + Target Profit) ÷ CM Ratio

🛡 Margin of Safety

Margin of Safety formulas
MOS in UnitsMOS = Actual Units − BEP Units
MOS in DollarsMOS $ = Actual Sales $ − BEP Sales $
MOS PercentageMOS% = MOS Units ÷ Actual Units × 100
InterpretationHigher MOS% = more cushion before losses begin

🔧 Operating Leverage

Operating Leverage formulas
Degree of Operating LeverageDOL = Total CM ÷ Net Operating Income
Profit sensitivity% Change in NOI = % Change in Sales × DOL
High DOLMore fixed costs → bigger profit swings
Low DOLMore variable costs → stable profit, lower risk
